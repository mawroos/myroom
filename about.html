About Us

My Room, voted one of Metro's Best Kids Stores for Clothing, Toys and Stuff in Auckland — we specialise in baby and children's trendy interior from nursery, first bedroom to big kids rooms as well as ensuring they look funky in out and about gear!

Starting off in 1995, trading as Nursery Tales, a brand created exclusively to produce prestige New Zealand made linen. 7 years later My Room was born as a store to stock Nursery Tales goods, as well as venturing into the market to stock only the best baby and children's products.

Shopping trips around the world are carried out to bring a point of difference to our store — we are passionate about the little ones and truly want to create a special place to play, sleep and eat.
